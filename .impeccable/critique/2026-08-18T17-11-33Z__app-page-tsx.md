## Design Specificity Verdict
- **Verdict:** Highly Bespoke PC Gaming Marketplace (9.2 / 10 Specificity).
- **Core Character:** Epic Games-style interactive video trailer switching, platform-native badges, ₹ INR pricing, dedicated "Deals under ₹299" shelf, UPI QR workflows, and prominent WhatsApp channel access.
- **Deterministic Findings:** 0 issues on `app/page.tsx`; 5 legacy CSS / bezier curve patterns identified across global styles and components.

## Priority Issues
1. **[P1] Platform Ambiguity on Multi-Platform Quick-Add:** Clicking quick-add on shelf cards defaults to `platforms[0]`. If a game has different pricing per platform, users might add the wrong edition.
2. **[P2] Shelf Fatigue & Repetition:** 7 vertical `GameShelf` sections with identical card grids can cause browsing fatigue.
3. **[P2] Micro-Copy Clarity:** "Add three more games to attain a code" is slightly cryptic on single game cards.
4. **[P3] Legacy Bezier Easing Curves:** 4 CSS animations in `otp-code-input.css` and `fly-to-cart-animator.tsx` use slight overshoot curves instead of smooth natural deceleration.

## Persona Red Flags
- **Alex (Power User):** Lacks upfront delivery-type indicators (Steam Key vs Offline Activation) on shelf cards.
- **Jordan (First-Timer):** May hesitate during UPI screenshot upload without visible average delivery ETA.
- **Casey (Mobile Gamer):** 7 stacked shelves require high scroll endurance; mobile trailer needs touch pass-through.
